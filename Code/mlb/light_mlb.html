Site Controls

Scoreboard

Left-click a team to stage a winner pick. Ctrl-click or Cmd-click a game to focus it. Middle-click, Alt-click, or press L while hovering a game to open the lineup card.

Lineups

The lineup card shows confirmed hitters, pitching, base state, count, recent team form, and the live play feed. Use the Lineups and Pitching tabs to switch views.

Player Tools

Ctrl-click or Cmd-click player pills and matchup entries to open player stat cards. The Leaders and Hot Players pages can be filtered by league, team, or today's opponents.

Home Run Rating

Home runs are scored on a 1000-point scale: walk-off impact up to 250, inning timing up to 120, game-result swing up to 180, runs scored up to 150, pitcher difficulty up to 200 based on HR/9 and IP reliability, and distance up to 100.

Bets And Goals

Build player prop or team win slips, track live bet status, export matchup data, and keep a timed focus objective in the dashboard panel.
